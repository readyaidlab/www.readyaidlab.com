— Why We Exist

Critical time can be lost before an ambulance arrives. ReadyAid is working to define Pre-ER: the gap in structured emergency response before emergency care reaches the patient.

01 — Problem

The critical Pre-ER gap

Before an ambulance arrives, every moment matters. Yet people without medical training may not know what to do next.

AEDs show what is possible. With the right system, people without medical training can help save lives.
— ReadyAid Thesis
02 — Principles

Four principles. One purpose.

Everything we build is guided by all four. These are our design principles.

— 01
Ready
Make readiness intuitive

Our design goal: enable an untrained person to begin use within 30 seconds. An intuitive experience, from opening the package to taking action.

— 02
Aid
Support the right action

Designed for meaningful clinical impact, not confidence alone. Guided by medical practice and relevant guidelines.

— 03
Certain
Mechanical reliability

A power-free, mechanical approach that avoids reliance on batteries or software. Designed with demanding environments in mind.

— 04
Guided
Measurable feedback

Feedback designed to indicate correct application in real time, helping users act with clarity and reducing the risk of misuse.
